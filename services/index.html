Our waterproofing services

Residential and commercial waterproofing across Canberra and Queanbeyan.

01

Bathroom & wet-area waterproofing

Showers, ensuites and laundries for new builds and renovations. We install membrane systems under the tiles and seal every junction, drain and hob to standard — so the wet area passes and stays dry for the life of the room.

02

Balcony & deck waterproofing

Exposed balconies and decks sitting over living areas take a beating from Canberra's weather. We seal them against rain and pooling water, with proper falls and detailing at the edges so nothing finds its way into the rooms below.

Call for a quote — 0418 629 185
03

Roof waterproofing & roofing systems

Membrane roofing systems for flat and low-pitch areas, plus roof repairs to stop active leaks. We track down where the water is really getting in and apply the right system to keep it out.

Call for a quote — 0418 629 185
04

Basement & below-ground waterproofing

Tanking and moisture protection for basements and below-ground structures. Getting this right at the structure stage keeps living and storage spaces dry and free of damp for good.

Call for a quote — 0418 629 185

Retaining wall waterproofing

Water sitting behind a retaining wall pushes through and damages the structure and anything behind it. We waterproof the wall properly so the water is managed and the wall lasts.

Driveway & surface waterproofing

Sealing trafficable and exposed surfaces so water runs off rather than soaking in. The right coating protects the surface underneath and the areas around it.

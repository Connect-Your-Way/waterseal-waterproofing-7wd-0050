Why it matters

Leaks don’t fix themselves — and they don’t get cheaper.

Water finds every weak point. Left alone, a failed seal in a shower, balcony or roof turns into rotten timber, blown tiles, stained ceilings and costly structural repairs. Waterseal Waterproofing installs and repairs waterproofing systems that keep water where it belongs — sealed, tested and built to last.

Serving Canberra and the Queanbeyan region — including Woden, Belconnen, Gungahlin, Tuggeranong, Weston Creek, the inner south and Queanbeyan.

What we do

From new membranes to fixing leaks that others missed.

Whether you’re renovating a bathroom, sealing a new balcony, or chasing a leak that keeps coming back, we prepare the surface properly, apply the right membrane system, and leave you with a watertight result. Careful prep, quality materials, and work done to Australian waterproofing standards.

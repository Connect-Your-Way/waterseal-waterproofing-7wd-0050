About Waterseal

Canberra’s waterproofing specialists.

Waterseal Waterproofing is a local Canberra waterproofing contractor working across the ACT and the Queanbeyan region. Waterproofing is all we do — bathrooms and wet areas, balconies and decks, roofs, basements, retaining walls, and remedial leak repairs, for both homes and businesses.

Our approach is simple: prepare the surface properly, use the right membrane system for the job, and check the work before we leave. It’s the difference between waterproofing that lasts and a leak you’ll be calling someone about again.
